About Me

Mathematics, research, storytelling, and public communication.

My academic background is in mathematics, with particular interests in topology, mathematical analysis, and structured problem-solving. My undergraduate thesis examined fuzzy topological spaces.

Outside academia, I produce long-form educational contents on Malaysia. My work combines archival research, source-based writing, visual storytelling, and public communication to make complex historical and political subjects more accessible.

I am especially interested in how history, institutions, economics, identity, and political choices shape contemporary Malaysia.
