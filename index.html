A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before we start.

Do I need to have a finished idea?

No. Start with the audience you want to reach and what you want them to understand. The project finder above can help you prepare a short brief.

Will the video be published on your channels?

For Malaysia projects, the professional documentary reference price starts from RM10,000 and includes channel publication. China and cross-border projects are quoted individually. Other collaborations can be production-only, channel publication, or both; platforms and deliverables are agreed in the proposal.

How is the project priced?

The quote depends on location, shoot days, research, editing, deliverables and usage rights. The Malaysia reference price does not automatically apply to China or cross-border projects. Currency, scope, revisions, schedule and payment arrangements are agreed before production.

Can you guarantee views or sales?

Views and sales depend on more than the film itself. We can agree on the message, production quality, deliverables and publication arrangements. Any performance figures used in a proposal will be based on actual data.
